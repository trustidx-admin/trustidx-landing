The problem

In the AI era, any document can be imitated, forged or backdated.

Contracts, diplomas, certificates, reports, invoices, but also photos, videos or audio files: the tools to produce convincing fakes have never been this accessible. The question is no longer whether a document looks genuine, but whether you can prove it. TrustIDX provides that proof.

How it works

A simple, public, permanent proof.

01

The fingerprint

Every file gets a unique cryptographic fingerprint: Word or Excel document, PDF, image, video, audio… any digital file, as is, with no conversion whatsoever. The file itself never leaves your organisation: only its fingerprint is used.

02

The anchoring

That fingerprint is recorded, timestamped, on Base, a public layer-2 blockchain secured by Ethereum. It is then recorded on Bitcoin, a second public ledger independent of the first. Once recorded, it cannot be tampered with.

03

The verification

Anyone can verify, freely and forever, that a document is authentic, dated, and genuinely comes from the organisation that issued it.

Why TrustIDX

A proof that does not depend on us.

The ledgers on which TrustIDX records your fingerprints are public: nobody controls them, nobody can rewrite them. What is certified today will remain verifiable tomorrow, with or without TrustIDX.

Two independent ledgers

Base carries the timestamped attestation and the provenance; Bitcoin attests the fingerprint's existence at a date. Two distinct records, on two ledgers that do not depend on each other.

Verifiable with public tooling

The records can be read with each ledger's public tooling: Ethereum Attestation Service for Base, OpenTimestamps for Bitcoin. No proprietary tool is required.

Privacy

Your data stays with you. Always.

Only your document's fingerprint is recorded on the public ledgers. Neither the file nor its content ever leaves your organisation.

What is a digital fingerprint?

A fingerprint (or hash) is a unique, fixed-length code computed from your file, much like a human fingerprint. Change a single comma or pixel, and the fingerprint becomes completely different. The document cannot be reconstructed from its fingerprint: the computation only works one way. And in practice, two different files cannot produce the same fingerprint. In short: the fingerprint proves everything about your document, yet reveals nothing of it.

The fingerprint is enough

No access to the source file is needed to certify it: holding its fingerprint is enough, along with any associated metadata. The one who certifies is not necessarily the one who holds the document.

Your file is never sent to us

The fingerprint is computed directly in your browser. The document itself is never uploaded: it passes through neither our servers nor the blockchain.

Your metadata belongs to you

You freely choose the metadata associated with a document. It is never published: only its fingerprint is anchored. Its integrity is proven, its content never exposed.

The prerequisite

One single prerequisite: your domain name.

Your domain is your identity. It is what proves the provenance of every certified document, publicly and unambiguously. If your organisation owns and controls a domain name, it is ready to certify.
